MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (SAMPLE)
This sample is dated 1 January 2025.

Parties
Acme Widgets LLC (“Disclosing Party”) and Northwind Review Co. (“Receiving Party”).

1. Confidential Information
“Confidential Information” means written information marked “Confidential” that the
Disclosing Party provides to the Receiving Party after the date of this sample.

2. Non-use and non-disclosure
The Receiving Party shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party
and shall use Confidential Information only to evaluate a possible commercial
discussion between the parties.

3. Duration of confidentiality obligations
The obligations in section 2 continue for two (2) years after the date the
Receiving Party receives the applicable Confidential Information.

4. Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that is publicly available
without breach of this sample, or that the Receiving Party already possessed
without a confidentiality duty.

5. No license
Nothing in this sample grants a license under any patent, copyright, or trademark.

6. No obligation to deal
Nothing in this sample requires either party to enter into any further agreement.

7. Governing text
This sample is governed by the written words on this page only. It does not
state a governing-law clause, a venue clause, or a remedy for breach.

END OF FICTIONAL SAMPLE
